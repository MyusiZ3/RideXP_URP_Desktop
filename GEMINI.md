# Project Rules & Customizations

## Strict Formatting & Tone Rules
- **No Emojis or Emoticons**: Do NOT use emojis, emoticons, or decorative Unicode symbols anywhere in chat responses, commit messages, documentation, or code comments.
- **Direct & Technical Tone**: Keep all explanations direct, professional, concise, and technical. Avoid AI slop, conversational fluff, or excessive decorations.
